# LRG
This repository contains the material for the Long Read Genomics courses. These courses are offered during Lecturer Mobilities (Erasmus) at different universities throughout Europe.

## History
- 2026: Autonomous University of Madrid (UAM), Spain
- 2026: University of Galway, Ireland
- 04/2026: University of Thessaly, Greece
- 11/2025: Czech University of Life Sciences Prague (CZU), Czechia



## Topics for courses at Autonomous University of Madrid (UAM)
- Brief introduction to nanopore sequencing
- Read mapping and visualization
- Hands-on session in the de.NBI cloud


## Topics for course at the University of Galway
- Lecture about sequencing technologies, genome sequence assembly and annotation
- Introduction to nanopore sequencing
- Read mapping and visualization
- De novo genome sequence assembly and evaluation
- Hands-on session in the de.NBI cloud


## Topics for lectures at University of Thessaly
- Sequencing technologies
- ONT-based plant genomics workflow
- Structural annotation
- Functional annotation
- Data submission

## Topics for lectures at Czech University of Life Sciences Prague (CZU)
- (1) Sequencing Technologies
- (2) Plant Genome Sequencing with ONT
- (3) Generating a Structural & Functional Annotation
- (4) Read Mapping and Variant Calling
- (5) Submitting Sequencing Data and Reusing Public Data
